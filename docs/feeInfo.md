**First National Bank Fee Schedule**

**Overview**

At First National Bank, we strive to provide our customers with competitive rates and services while maintaining a fair and transparent fee structure.

**Fees**

1. **Monthly Maintenance Fee**
* Applies to Basic Savings accounts with balances below $100
* Waived if minimum balance requirement is met ($100)
* $5 per month
2. **Overdraft Fee**
* $25 for each overdraft transaction
* $50 for each subsequent overdraft transaction within a 30-day period
3. ** NSF (Non-Sufficient Funds) Fee**
* $30 for each NSF transaction
4. **Large Withdrawal Fee**
* $10 for withdrawals exceeding $5,000 per day
5. **Out-of-State ATM Fee**
* $5 for out-of-state ATMs
6. **International Transaction Fee**
* 1% of the transaction amount for international transactions

**Other Fees**

1. **Account Closure Fee**: $25 for closing an account within the first six (6) months of opening
2. **Inactivity Fee**: $10 per quarter for accounts with no activity for 12 consecutive months
3. **Paper Statement Fee**: $5 per month for paper statements

**Waivers and Credits**

1. **Minimum Balance Requirement**: Meet the minimum balance requirement to waive the monthly maintenance fee
2. **Direct Deposit**: Receive direct deposit to waive the monthly maintenance fee
3. **Student Account**: Students with a valid .edu email address may have their monthly maintenance fee waived

**Dispute Resolution**

If you believe you have been incorrectly charged or denied a service, please contact our customer service department and we will investigate your claim.
